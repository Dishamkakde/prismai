Who We Are

About PRISM AI

The Artificial Intelligence Club of RNS Institute of Technology, Bengaluru.

Our Story

A Student-Led AI Community

PRISM AI was founded by a group of AI enthusiasts who believed students shouldn't wait for industry to start solving real-world problems. We created a space where curious minds can explore, experiment, and build together.

From machine learning models to generative AI systems and computer vision tools — our members work on projects that push boundaries, guided by structured research and peer collaboration.

Our Mission

Democratizing AI at RNSIT

To make AI research and development accessible, structured and impactful for every student — regardless of prior experience. We provide the tools, mentorship, and community to help you grow.

Research First

Every project begins with structured research — literature reviews, gap analysis, and hypothesis formulation before any code is written.

Team-Driven

Great AI is built by great teams. Every member is valued, mentored, and recognized for their contributions.

Growth-Oriented

Track your contributions, earn XP, unlock badges, and build a portfolio that speaks for itself.
